# Jadavpur University, Kolkata

## B.E. / B.Tech Programs
Duration: 4 years  

### Eligibility
- Must pass Class 12 with:
  - Physics
  - Chemistry
  - Mathematics
- Must obtain:
  - Minimum 60% in PCM combined
  - 45% for reserved categories.

### Entrance Exam
- Admission based on rank in WBJEE.
- Seat allotted through counselling.

### Admission Timeline
- UG admission starts after Class 12 results are declared.

### Fees
- B.Tech tuition range:
  - Approximately ₹9,600 to ₹1.21 lakh total.

### Financial Support
- Free studentships and financial aid available.

### Campus
Main campus

Jadavpur University main campus map (as of 2015)
Jadavpur University's main campus is at Jadavpur, spread over 60 acres. The campus contains major engineering, arts, science and interdisciplinary departments. The 6 Acres sports complex have Badminton court, Tennis court, Kabaddi court and playground for other organising sports like cricket and football. Additionally the university has a six canteens, amenities center, and guest house, cafeteria, health centre, gymnasium, Pwd centre and other facilities like banks, post office, book shops etc.[1] Although every department/ school has its own hall or auditorium, the main campus have 7 auditoriums: Gandhi Bhavan, Triguna Sen Auditorium, K.P. Basu Memorial Hall, Vivekananda Hall, Anita Banerjee Memorial Hall and H.L. Roy Auditorium. The campus also has an Open Air Theatre with a seating capacity of around 3000.
